VoidDownload
  • A draft is deleted; an issued invoice is voided. A draft holds no number, so deleting it costs nothing. Once issued the number is on record and only voiding is offered — that is what keeps numbering gapless. The UI shows one and never the other.
  • Downloading is the primary action, in every status. With no email, the download is how an invoice reaches a client.
