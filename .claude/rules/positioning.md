## Before writing a word of product copy or product doc

`docs/positioning.md` owns the thesis, the competitors and the price. Read it
before changing what this product claims to be. Where it and another doc
disagree, it wins and the other is wrong.

**Time tracking and invoicing for one contractor.** Tracking is free and
complete; downloading an invoice is the paid tier, at $15/month or $120/year.

**Three advantages, and they are structural** — a competitor cannot copy them
without giving something up. Every one of them is paid per seat, which makes
them hostile to one person. The invoice is the product and the timer is the
input, because five competitors give tracking away. The price is knowable in
advance and stays.

**Restraint is not the pitch.** "We do less" is a claim about us, costs a free
Toggl user nothing, and every competitor makes it. We do not build team
features because there is no team — that is a consequence, not a virtue.

**Do not out-feature anyone.** Zoho Invoice is free forever and has more
features than we do. Parity with a venture-funded incumbent is not available
and is not the goal.

## What a claim costs

**One claim, one doc.** `CLAUDE.md`'s routing table says which. Two files
saying the same thing means an edit to one leaves the other silently wrong,
and nothing detects it — so before writing a rationale, check whether the
doc that owns it already has one and point at it instead.

**A doc states the current final form.** Git holds the history: no "this used
to say", no decision log, no record of what was turned down.

## What gets built

**Nothing enters `docs/roadmap.md` without passing the gate at the top of that
file** — whose problem, what breaks without it, one person or a team we do not
have, which milestone. Annoyance does not make the roadmap. A known fault goes
to `docs/defects.md` instead, where it needs no justification.

**A past rejection does not bind.** The thesis moved in September 2026 and
there is no archive of what was declined. Re-argue the idea against
`positioning.md` rather than looking up whether it was refused before.

## The unvalidated part

Everything above is **a bet, not a finding** — `positioning.md`'s last section
says what would settle it. Write the thesis as what we are building on, never
as something users have confirmed.
