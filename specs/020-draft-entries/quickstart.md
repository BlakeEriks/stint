# Quickstart: A draft lists the entries it bills

1. `pnpm seed builder@localhost.test`, sign in, open a draft.
2. Below the invoice, read Entries: date, task, project, hours, amount.
3. Press a row, change its end time, save: the row, the lines and the total
   change.
4. Open a sent invoice: its entries show hours, no amount, and don't open.
