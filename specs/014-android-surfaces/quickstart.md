# Quickstart: The timer outside the Android app

On the PR's preview build (`docs/mobile.md`, Preview builds), signed in:

1. Add Stint's widget in both sizes, and the Stint tile to Quick Settings.
2. Start the timer on the web: the notification, widgets and tile show it
   within 5 seconds; on Android 16 the status bar shows its clock.
3. Press Stop on the notification: every surface shows it stopped at once.
4. Press Start on a widget: it begins the most recent task.
5. Turn on airplane mode and press the tile: after 10 seconds it turns back,
   and the widget names why.
