## Before writing a word of product copy or product doc

**Read `docs/positioning.md`.** It owns the thesis, the competitors and the
price, and where it and another doc disagree it wins. Do not restate it here
or anywhere else — point at it.

Two things it cannot say about itself, because they are about how to write
rather than what is true:

**Restraint is not the pitch.** "We do less" costs a reader on someone else's
free plan nothing, and every competitor claims it. We have no team features
because there is no team — a consequence, not a virtue. Sell that the invoice
is right and the bill does not move.

**It is a bet, not a finding.** Write the thesis as what we are building on,
never as something users have confirmed. `positioning.md`'s last section says
what would settle it.
